ToolScope is an independent publication that reviews software the way careful engineers actually evaluate it: by defining what matters, testing against it, and explaining the reasoning in plain language.

## Why this publication exists

Choosing software is harder than it should be. Marketing pages promise everything, sponsored posts bury the trade-offs, and community threads contradict each other. We built ToolScope to be the publication we wanted to read: small in scope, rigorous in method, and explicit about how every conclusion was reached.

## What we cover

- **AI tools** — assistants, coding agents, writing tools, image generators, and productivity AI.
- **Developer software** — IDEs, databases, hosting platforms, APIs, and SaaS infrastructure.
- **General software** — PDF editors, note-taking apps, screen recorders, and alternatives to everyday tools.

## How we stay independent

Our revenue comes from display advertising and affiliate commissions. Neither changes what we recommend:

- We publish our [testing methodology](/en/testing-methodology) and hold every review to it.
- Affiliate relationships, when they exist, are disclosed on the page where the link appears and in our [affiliate disclosure](/en/affiliate-disclosure).
- No vendor sees a review before publication, and no vendor can pay to change a verdict.

## Who is behind it

ToolScope is written and maintained by an independent editor based in Algeria, publishing in English and Arabic. The site is deliberately small: fewer reviews, each of them tested and documented, rather than a large catalog of summaries.

## The publication in three commitments

1. **Every claim is checkable.** If we say a tool handles a workflow well, we describe the workflow we tested.
2. **Nothing is fabricated.** If we have not tested something, we say so instead of publishing an invented score.
3. **Readers come first.** We recommend the tool we would choose with our own money, including the free one.
