ToolScope is reader-supported. This page explains exactly what that means.

## The short version

Some links on this site are **affiliate links**. If you click one and buy or subscribe, we may earn a commission from the provider. **You never pay more** because of it — in some cases you pay less, when we can negotiate reader discounts.

## How affiliate links are marked

- Affiliate links carry `rel="sponsored nofollow"` and an external-link indicator.
- Any article containing affiliate links shows a disclosure notice near the links themselves — not just in the footer.
- Links to a provider's official website that are *not* affiliate links are plain links.

## What affiliate money never does

- It does not determine which products we review, or which one wins a comparison.
- It does not give vendors early access to, or any influence over, our reviews — see the [editorial policy](/en/editorial-policy).
- It does not change our testing methodology or the evidence we publish.

## Current status

As of the date above, **no affiliate programs are active** on ToolScope. The site's link infrastructure is built and ready — when a program is joined, it will appear in this disclosure with its start date, per product.

## Display advertising

We plan to run limited display advertising:

- Ads will always be labeled and visually distinct from editorial content.
- We do not run ads that mimic download buttons, navigation, or article controls.
- Advertising partners have no influence on editorial decisions.
