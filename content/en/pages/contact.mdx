## What to contact us about

- **Corrections** — if something in a review is wrong or outdated, tell us. We fix errors visibly and credit substantive correction requests.
- **Review suggestions** — tell us which tools you want tested and what decision you're trying to make. Reader requests shape the testing queue.
- **Partnerships and advertising** — see the [affiliate disclosure](/en/affiliate-disclosure) for our terms. We don't sell reviews or verdicts.
- **Press** — for quotes or commentary on software and AI tooling.

## What we don't do

- We don't accept guest posts at this time.
- We don't provide personalized purchasing advice by email, but our [guides](/en/guides) cover the common decisions.
