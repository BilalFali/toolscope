## Two paths to the same problem

Both Tailscale and ZeroTier solve the same underlying problem: let a group of devices reach each other privately and encrypted, without a traditional VPN's subnets, static IPs, and gateway boxes. They differ less in what they promise and more in how billing and self-hosting are structured.

## Pricing, compared directly

| | Tailscale | ZeroTier |
|---|---|---|
| Free tier | Up to 6 users, unlimited devices | Up to 10 devices, 1 network |
| First paid tier | $8/user/month (Standard) | $18/month flat for 10 devices (Essential) |
| Billing model | Per user | Per device, in tiers |
| Top tier | Premium, $18/user/month | Scale, $179/month for 100 devices |
| Custom/enterprise | Yes (contact sales) | Yes — two tiers: Enterprise and Quantum (post-quantum crypto) |

Source: [Tailscale pricing](https://tailscale.com/pricing) and [ZeroTier pricing](https://www.zerotier.com/pricing) as published on 2026-10-27.

The practical effect: Tailscale's free tier is more generous for a *small number of people* with many devices each (unlimited devices per user). ZeroTier's free tier is more generous for a *fixed device count* regardless of how many people share it — useful for, say, a homelab or a handful of servers rather than a growing headcount.

## Self-hosting

This is the most-cited structural difference, and it's worth stating precisely: ZeroTier's own pricing page doesn't advertise a specific self-hosting price or plan — it links to a self-hosted EULA and docs, describing deployment "within your own infrastructure." Tailscale does not offer an official self-hosted control plane at all; the common path is the independent, open-source **Headscale** project, which is not a Tailscale product and carries its own maintenance burden. Neither claim should be taken as "fully self-hostable out of the box" without reading each project's current docs.

## Security and compliance tiers

ZeroTier's higher tiers (Enterprise and Quantum) explicitly advertise FIPS 140-3 validated cryptography, and Quantum adds post-quantum cryptography (CNSA 2.0). Tailscale's Enterprise tier is quoted as custom, without a published equivalent claim on the pricing page at the time of writing. Teams with specific compliance requirements should verify current certifications directly with each vendor rather than relying on this summary.

## Who should look at which

If you're a small team (6 or fewer people) who wants the fastest setup with no device-count anxiety, Tailscale's free tier is the more natural starting point. If your constraint is a fixed number of devices — a handful of servers, a lab, a single office — rather than headcount, ZeroTier's device-based free and paid tiers may map onto your actual usage more cleanly. Neither comparison point is a performance claim: this article does not measure latency, throughput, or connection reliability for either product.

## What would make this comparison more useful

A hands-on follow-up would time setup on a clean machine for both tools, measure throughput between two representative nodes, and document what self-hosting Headscale or a ZeroTier controller actually requires in practice. Until that cycle runs, treat this as a pricing and feature map, not a performance verdict.
