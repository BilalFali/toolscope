## Why look beyond Tailscale

Tailscale's free tier caps at 6 users, and its official hosted control plane is proprietary — there's no first-party option to run the coordination server yourself. Common reasons to look elsewhere:

- **You need official self-hosting.** Tailscale's own pricing page doesn't offer a self-hosted control plane; the common workaround is the unofficial Headscale project.
- **Your constraint is devices, not people.** A small number of people managing many servers may fit ZeroTier's device-based tiers more cleanly than Tailscale's per-user billing.
- **You want a fully open-source stack**, client and server, without relying on a single vendor's hosted service.

## The alternatives

### ZeroTier

ZeroTier's free tier allows up to 10 devices on a single network — no login/user cap, which suits small, device-heavy setups like homelabs or a handful of production servers. Paid tiers (Essential at $18/month for 10 devices, Scale at $179/month for 100) bill per device tier rather than per user. The top tiers (Enterprise, Quantum) add FIPS 140-3 validated cryptography, and Quantum specifically adds post-quantum cryptography — relevant if you have a concrete compliance requirement, not a general recommendation.

**Read our [Tailscale vs ZeroTier comparison](/en/compare/tailscale-vs-zerotier) for the full pricing breakdown.**

### NetBird

NetBird markets itself as open source with an "Open Source" badge linking to its GitHub repository, and its pricing page explicitly describes self-hosting as deploying "within your own infrastructure for full control and flexibility," alongside a Self-Hosted EULA and documentation. Its hosted free tier covers up to 5 users and 100 machines; hosted paid tiers (Team at €6/user/month, Business at €12/user/month) add SSO, SCIM, and device posture checks. The hosted and self-hosted paths are genuinely separate decisions — verify current self-hosting requirements in NetBird's own docs before committing engineering time to it.

### Headscale

Headscale isn't a company or a product with pricing — it's a community-maintained, open-source reimplementation of Tailscale's coordination server, designed to work with Tailscale's own official client software. It's the closest thing to "self-host Tailscale itself," but it is explicitly not an official Tailscale product, and the project's docs are the only authoritative source on its current feature parity and limitations. This fits teams with the engineering capacity to run and maintain their own coordination server, not teams looking for a vendor-supported alternative.

## How to choose

| Priority | Best fit |
|---|---|
| Official vendor-supported self-hosting | NetBird |
| Device-based pricing instead of per-user | ZeroTier |
| Keep Tailscale's client, self-host the server | Headscale |
| Simplest hosted setup, fewer than 6 users | Stay with Tailscale |
