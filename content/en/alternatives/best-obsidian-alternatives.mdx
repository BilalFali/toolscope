## Why look beyond Obsidian

Obsidian's local-file model is a deliberate trade-off, not a universal best. Common reasons to consider something else:

- **You need real collaboration now**, not a paid add-on bolted onto a single-player tool.
- **You think in outlines, not documents** — Obsidian's editor is file- and document-oriented; some people prefer a block/outliner model from the start.
- **You want sync included in a free, open-source app**, rather than choosing and paying for Obsidian's official Sync service or configuring a third-party alternative yourself.

## The alternatives

### Notion

Notion bundles hosting, sync, and real-time collaboration into one per-member price, starting at $10/member/month on the Plus plan, with a Free tier available for individuals. Its database model (properties, relations, multiple views) goes beyond note-taking into lightweight app-building. The trade-off is the one described in our [Obsidian vs Notion comparison](/en/compare/obsidian-vs-notion): your content lives in a hosted database, not a folder of files you control directly.

### Logseq

Logseq is open source and local-first, storing notes as Markdown (and Org-mode) files on disk — similar in spirit to Obsidian's portability promise, but built around an outliner/block model rather than Obsidian's document-first editor. Its plugin ecosystem and canvas features are smaller than Obsidian's, per third-party comparisons we reviewed, though neither claim was independently verified by us. If the outliner model (bullets and nesting, rather than freeform long-form writing) matches how you already think, it's worth trialing directly — confirm current sync pricing on logseq.com, since we could not verify a reliable figure.

### Joplin

Joplin is a free, open-source note app with sync built into the core product rather than sold as a separate add-on — you choose a sync backend (a self-hosted server, or a supported third-party cloud provider), and costs depend entirely on which backend you pick. This makes it the most directly "free, with sync included" option of the three, at the cost of a less mature plugin ecosystem than Obsidian's.

## How to choose

| Priority | Best fit |
|---|---|
| Native team collaboration today | Notion |
| Outliner/block editing model, still local-first | Logseq |
| Free app with sync included, not sold separately | Joplin |
| Maximum plugin ecosystem, document-first editing | Stay with Obsidian |
